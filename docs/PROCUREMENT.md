# Procurement Operating System

TextileHub transitions buyers and suppliers from a simple marketplace into a comprehensive B2B Procurement Operating System (OS).

## Kanban Workspace
The core of the procurement workflow is the Procurement Workspace (`/dashboard/procurement`).
Instead of static lists, users manage RFQs and Orders through an intuitive Kanban board.

### Buyer Flow
1. **Draft RFQs**: Incomplete or saved RFQs.
2. **Submitted**: RFQs sent to suppliers awaiting review.
3. **Quote Received**: Supplier has provided pricing and terms.
4. **Negotiating**: Ongoing counter-offers.
5. **Purchase Orders**: Accepted quotes converted to active orders.
6. **In Production**: Manufacturing tracking.
7. **Shipping & Delivered**: Logistics milestones.

### Supplier Flow (Sales Pipeline)
1. **Incoming RFQs**: New leads.
2. **Draft Quotes**: Estimating costs.
3. **Negotiating**: Countering buyer offers.
4. **Won**: Converted to Purchase Orders.
5. **Production & Completed**: Fulfilling the order.

## Analytics
Buyers and Suppliers have access to a Recharts-powered dashboard tracking:
- **Spend & Savings**: Tracking YTD spend and AI-negotiated savings.
- **Lead Times**: Averages across different suppliers.
- **Activity Feed**: Real-time notifications regarding the supply chain.
