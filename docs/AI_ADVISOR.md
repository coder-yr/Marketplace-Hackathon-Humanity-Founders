# AI Procurement Advisor

The AI Procurement Advisor transforms raw quotes into actionable enterprise intelligence.

## Features

### 1. Cost Insights
- **Market Benchmarking**: AI compares the quoted price against historical market data for similar GSM/Materials.
- **Target Price**: Recommends a fair target price to negotiate toward.

### 2. Negotiation Strategy
- **Counter-Offer Generation**: Suggests an optimal counter-offer price based on order volume and market conditions.
- **Rationale**: Provides bullet-pointed reasons (e.g., "Your order volume of 5000m warrants a 4% volume discount") to use in negotiations.

### 3. Supplier Risk Analysis
- **Risk Scoring**: Evaluates the supplier as Low, Medium, or High risk.
- **Factors**: Analyzes response rate, ISO certification validity, dispute history, and delivery consistency.

### 4. Alternative Suppliers
- If a quote is unfavorable, the AI instantly suggests 2-3 alternative verified suppliers capable of fulfilling the exact same RFQ requirements with match percentages.

## Enterprise Copilot (Cmd+K)
The AI is accessible globally via the Cmd+K shortcut, enabling users to:
- Instantly search for suppliers ("Find organic cotton mills in Turkey")
- Navigate the OS (Jump to Analytics, Orders, Activity)
- Act as a command palette for rapid procurement workflows.
