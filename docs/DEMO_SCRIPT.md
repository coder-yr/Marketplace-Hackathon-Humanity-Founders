# Demo Script — B2B Textile Marketplace

*Last updated: Phase 0 — Foundation*

---

## Hackathon Demo Overview

**Total Demo Time:** 5 minutes  
**Audience:** Hackathon judges  
**Goal:** Show a complete buyer-supplier interaction with AI-powered discovery

---

## Demo Flow (Planned — Phase 5)

### 0:00 — Hook (30 seconds)
> "The global textile industry is a $1.7 trillion market. But buyers and suppliers still find each other through trade shows, WhatsApp groups, and cold calls. We built the platform they deserve."

- Show the landing page
- Highlight the AI search bar

---

### 0:30 — Supplier Experience (90 seconds)
1. Login as Supplier (pre-seeded account)
2. Show the supplier dashboard
3. Create a product listing — Cotton Fabric 40s Count
   - Fill in name, description, MOQ, price
   - Upload a fabric image
   - Tag with categories (Cotton, Premium)
4. Product appears in catalog

---

### 2:00 — Buyer Experience with AI (2 minutes)
1. Login as Buyer (different account)
2. Use the **AI Search** bar: type *"soft breathable fabric for summer shirts"*
3. Watch AI find the Cotton 40s product even without exact keyword match
4. Open product detail page
5. Click **Request Quote**
6. Fill in MOQ, message → Submit RFQ

---

### 4:00 — Supplier Responds (45 seconds)
1. Switch to supplier view
2. See the incoming RFQ notification
3. Accept and send a quote
4. Show the conversation thread

---

### 4:45 — Closing (15 seconds)
> "Built with React, Node.js, MongoDB, and Hugging Face AI. Deployed on Vercel and Render. Ready to scale."

- Show the stack slide
